Magnetic Math Universe (MMU) is a hardware-level computational model where numbers are treated as physical active **Energies** with magnetic polarities rather than passive binary states ($0$ and $1$). Designed as a hardware-level thought experiment, MMU explores constant-space $O(1)$ search-space pruning for non-deterministic polynomial problems via spontaneous topology collapse.
